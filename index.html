Last Words

About 40% of the world's languages are endangered. When the last speakers go, their words go with them. Last Words is a small skeleton for building apps that keep those words alive, in the voices of the people who speak them.

🪦 Ghost Words

Learn real recorded words from Breton, Irish, Welsh and Basque, straight from Wikidata. Every word you learn rises from its grave.

📼 Echo Archive

Record your own language, word by word, with the speaker's consent and a license they choose. Share it as one file, or practice it in Ghost Words.

One skeleton, two apps

skeleton/src/
  pack.ts      the language pack format + validation of untrusted files
  lexemes.ts   packs from Wikidata lexemes with recordings
  srs.ts       Leitner spaced repetition
  quiz.ts      multiple-choice questions with distinct answers
  audio.ts     playback (incl. Commons MP3 fallback), recording, waveforms
  store.ts     IndexedDB for packs, recordings and progress
  ui.tsx       PlayButton, Waveform
ghost-words/   app 1: learner      (uses lexemes, srs, quiz, audio, store, ui)
echo-archive/  app 2: archivist    (uses pack, audio, store, ui)

Both apps share one pack format and one on-device store, so a word recorded in Echo Archive can be practiced in Ghost Words a second later. Source on GitHub.
