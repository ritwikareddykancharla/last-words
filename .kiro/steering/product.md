# Product: Last Words

Last Words is a skeleton for endangered-language apps, shown with two distinct apps: **Ghost Words** (learn real recorded words; learned words rise from their graves) and **Echo Archive** (record a language word by word with consent). Kiroween 2026, **Skeleton Crew** category.

## Who it's for
- Developers and community groups who want to build something for a fading language without starting from zero.
- Learners and heritage speakers (Ghost Words); elders, teachers and archivists (Echo Archive).

## Pillars
1. **Real voices only.** Every word comes with a recording by a real speaker, credited. No text-to-speech for endangered languages.
2. **Consent and ownership.** Communities own their recordings. Explicit consent per recording, a license chosen by the speaker, on-device by default, warnings before sharing restricted recordings.
3. **One format, many apps.** Everything goes through the `LanguagePack` format, so apps interoperate.
4. **Works without an account or server.**

## Tone and look
- Each app has its own identity on the same skeleton: Ghost Words is a moonlit graveyard; Echo Archive is a warm attic of tapes and typewriter labels.
- Spooky but tender. The theme is about bringing words back, never mocking loss.

## Non-goals
- Machine translation or AI-generated pronunciations.
- Hosting community recordings on a server in v1.
